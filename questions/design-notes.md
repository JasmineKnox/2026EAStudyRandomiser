# Question presentation review — 15 October 2026

The questions remain original revision practice with indicative marks. They are not QCAA-endorsed instruments, complete examination papers or reproductions of sample questions. Public IA1 examples informed question construction; IA1 content has not been substituted for EA content.

## Worked solutions

All maths and science solutions use an ordered sequence of labelled steps. Each equation, substitution and result occupies a separate line. Units, rounding, direction and scientific interpretation are included where relevant. Conceptual science responses show a reasoning sequence rather than an artificial formula template. Several calculation questions in other subjects use the same layout. Multi-part stems separate the available information and each instruction.

`working` is the displayed solution when present; `answer` retains the prose reference answer. Update both if the substantive solution changes. The renderer uses text nodes and a semantic ordered list, so the data remains safe and accessible without a maths-rendering service.

## Legal Studies

Reviewed [IA1 sample](https://www.qcaa.qld.edu.au/downloads/senior-qce/hass/snr_legal_studies_25_ia1_smple_ass_inst_exam.pdf) and [2026 endorsement report, IA1 advice](https://www.qcaa.qld.edu.au/downloads/senior-qce/hass/snr_legal_studies_26_subj_rpt_endorse.pdf).

Prompts identify the governance context and use direct commands. The extended-response skills item identifies the issue, supplies contrasting fictional sources, and asks for two viewpoints and their consequences, legal alternatives, a recommendation justified by legal criteria, and an implication. It is labelled a skills exercise, not a full IA1 item. The 2026 report takes precedence where its advice refines the earlier sample’s wording. No IA1 word count is imposed.

## Economics

Reviewed [IA1 sample](https://www.qcaa.qld.edu.au/downloads/senior-qce/hass/snr_economics_25_unit3_ia1_smple_ass_inst.pdf) and [2026 endorsement report, IA1 advice](https://www.qcaa.qld.edu.au/downloads/senior-qce/hass/snr_economics_26_subj_rpt_endorse.pdf).

Calculations and explanations are separate instructions. Unit 3 practice retains its international-trade scope. Evaluation wording uses an economic criterion and a justified decision. The EA policy item lets the student choose one criterion; its model demonstrates employment rather than prescribing that criterion in the question.

## Business

Reviewed [IA1 sample](https://www.qcaa.qld.edu.au/downloads/senior-qce/hass/snr_business_25_unit3_ia1_smple_ass_inst.pdf) and [2026 endorsement report, IA1 advice](https://www.qcaa.qld.edu.au/downloads/senior-qce/hass/snr_business_26_subj_rpt_endorse.pdf).

Questions name the analytical task or evaluation criteria and require a justified decision and recommendation where appropriate. Unit 3 and EA contexts remain distinct. These small drills do not claim to reproduce the complete IA1 context and stimulus requirements.

## Geography and other humanities

Reviewed [Geography IA1 sample](https://www.qcaa.qld.edu.au/downloads/senior-qce/hass/snr_geography_25_ia1_smple_ass_inst.pdf).

Separate calculations, interpretation and explanation into clearly labelled parts. History, Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Studies and religion prompts now use direct commands with explicit evidence requirements. Hypothetical sources remain labelled; they are not presented as authentic historical or religious documents.
